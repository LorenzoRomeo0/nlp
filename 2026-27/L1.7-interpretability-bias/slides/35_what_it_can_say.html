What looking inside can and cannot tell us.

What we saw in our storiesWhat it does not tell us
Attribution
The decision to stay silent rests on what the text says about the friend’s happiness, not on the country: removing every Moroccan marker, or changing the country in the prompt, barely moves the odds.
Why the model “chooses”. It shows what this output depends on, in this text. Our gradient variants failed their own check and are not shown.
Probing
The decision becomes readable only as the story approaches it. Only near the end do the activations beat a word counter, and by a little.
That the model uses what a probe reads, or that it “already knew”. Decodable is not used.
Interventions
A direction found in our own stories turns the decision from about 94% silence to about 97% telling with a push of four units, and the stated reasons follow.
That it is “the” place of the decision, or a moral signal. The same push changes the protagonist’s gender, the reasons and the length of the story; other directions move it too, less. A handle, not a map.

Looking inside gives us more to argue with, not a verdict on a model’s morality.

Qwen2.5-32B-Instruct, one dilemma, one language-model judge. Interventions: layer 36, four prompts, about 100 stories per setting; “about 94% silence” and “about 97% telling” are the averages at α = −4 and α = +4 over the four prompts.
