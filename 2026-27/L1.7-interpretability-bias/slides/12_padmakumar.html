Homogenisation · Padmakumar & He (2024)

Writing with a tuned model makes essays more alike.

  • The test
    38 writers produced 300 argumentative essays on 10 topics: alone, with the base GPT-3, or with InstructGPT, tuned with human feedback.
  • The finding
    With InstructGPT, essays are less diverse in wording and in content. With the base model the difference is not significant.
  • Whose words
    The loss comes from the model’s contributions; the parts written by people keep their diversity.
  • The reading
    Alignment with human feedback may favour homogenisation, as a side effect of making answers better.
0.154 → 0.166
similarity between essays (ROUGE-L), writing alone versus with InstructGPT; with GPT-3, 0.158 (not significant)
0.20 vs 0.11
similarity among the model’s suggestions: InstructGPT versus GPT-3

Limits: short argumentative essays, 2023 models, single sessions; no long-term effects, no other genres.

Padmakumar, V. & He, H. (2024). Does Writing with Language Models Reduce Content Diversity? ICLR 2024. arxiv.org/abs/2309.05196
