Homogenisation · Chakrabarty et al. (2024)

Fluent is not the same as creative.

  • The test
    14 yes/no tests of creative writing, devised with experts; 12 New Yorker stories against 36 written by three models from the same plots.
  • The finding
    Professional stories pass most tests; the models’ stories are 3 to 10 times less likely to pass each one.
  • Models differ
    GPT-4 does relatively better on originality; Claude 1.3 on fluency, flexibility and elaboration.
  • Models as judges
    Asked to take the same tests, the models do not agree with the experts: no meaningful positive correlation.
