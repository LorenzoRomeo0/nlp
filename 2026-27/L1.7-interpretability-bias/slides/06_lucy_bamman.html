Representation bias · Lucy & Bamman (2021)

GPT-3 gives women and men different story worlds.

  • The test
    Openings of 402 contemporary novels (2,154 characters) as prompts; GPT-3 continues them and the topics of the stories are measured.
  • The finding
    Women are tied to family, emotions and appearance; men to politics, war, technology and crime. Women are also written as less powerful, even when the prompt shows them acting with authority.
  • The same prompt
    In 7,334 pairs of stories from an identical prompt, GPT-3 gave the protagonist a different gender, and the story developed differently.
  • Correcting by prompting
    It can raise how intelligent women appear, but power stereotypes are much harder to move.

Lucy, L. & Bamman, D. (2021). Gender and Representation Bias in GPT-3 Generated Stories. Proceedings of the Third Workshop on Narrative Understanding (NAACL), pp. 48–55. ACL. aclanthology.org/2021.nuse-1.5
