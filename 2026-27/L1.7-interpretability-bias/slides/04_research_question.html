The research question

How do language models represent human culture and the domain of moral choice, and how pluralist is that representation in the language they use?

Hypotheses to test along the way
H1
A country name changes the scenery more than the story.
Names, places and objects follow the country; what the protagonist decides, and why, changes far less.
Our tests
H2
Culture enters as a statement about the country, not as the characters’ own reasons.
When values are tied to a country they are asserted in general terms, while the reasons that drive the choice stay largely the same.
Our tests
H3
Models know more than they apply.
They answer questions about a culture correctly, yet misrepresent it when they narrate.
What the literature shows
H4
Post-training narrows what models say.
Part of the loss of diversity comes from tuning models towards what raters prefer, not only from the data they learn from.
