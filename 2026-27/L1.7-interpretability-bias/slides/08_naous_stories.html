Representation bias · Naous et al. (2024)

Arab names are written as poorer and more traditional.

  • The test
    Stories about characters with Arab or Western names; the adjectives used for each group are counted and compared.
  • The finding
    Arab names go with poverty, traditionalism and religiosity; Western names with wealth, prestige and likeable traits.
  • Can prompting fix it?
    Only partly. Naming the culture in the prompt has a limited effect; few-shot examples drawn from Arab culture generally reduce the bias.
  • Why it matters
    It is a matter of conditional probability: even when the prompt gives explicit cultural information, Western associations stay more likely.
