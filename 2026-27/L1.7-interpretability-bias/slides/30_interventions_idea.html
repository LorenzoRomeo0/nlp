Looking for the why · 3 Interventions

Interventions ask what changes if we alter it.

The question
While the model writes
add a direction to its internal activations, with strength α
↓
Then look at the behaviour
does the decision of the story move with α?

If we turn the knob, does the behaviour follow?

How it works
  1. 1Find a directionFor example, the difference between the average activations of stories that tell and stories that stay silent.
  2. 2Push along it, in both senses, with growing strengthAdded at one layer, at every word the model reads and writes.
  3. 3Measure the behaviour, against controlsA random direction of the same size should do nothing; a direction for an unrelated property should move that property, not this one.
What it shows that a direction is causally involved, if the behaviour follows. What it does not show that it is the only place, or that it is “the” cause: a handle, not a map.
