Probing asks what the model represents, and where.

The question
Inside the model
layer 64…layer k: 5,120 numbers at one word…layer 1
↓
A simple classifier
reads one property from those numbers: what the story will decide?

Is the property in there, and from which layer on?

How it works
  1. 1Pick a property we can labelFor example, the decision each story ends up making.
  2. 2Record the model’s internal activationsAt one layer and one point of the text, for thousands of texts.
  3. 3Train a classifier on them, test it on unseen textsIf it reads the property well, the information is present at that layer. Controls: the same classifier on the visible text, and on a property that should not be there.
What it shows that information is present and where. What it does not show that the model uses it: decodable is not the same as used.
