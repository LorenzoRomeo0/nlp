The limits
  1. One model, one dilemma. A single open-weights model and a single moral scene, written freely.
  2. One judge. A single family of language models, without systematic human validation, and with uneven knowledge of each culture.
  3. Reasons on request. The story is asked to say why: these are reasons produced, not behaviour observed.
  4. Names leak the country. Places and personal names in a story can reveal it even to the blind judge.
  5. Failures kept. 106 stories (2.3%) drift into Chinese midway; we keep and count them, and analyse the other 4,489.
