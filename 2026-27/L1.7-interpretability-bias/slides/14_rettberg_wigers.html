Homogenisation · Rettberg & Wigers (2025)

Different countries, the same story.

  • The test
    GPT-4o-mini asked for “a 1500 word potential {demonym} story”: 50 stories for each of 236 nationalities, plus 50 with no nationality. Lexical analysis, emotions, and close reading of the American, Norwegian, Israeli and Palestinian ones.
  • The pattern
    A protagonist faces a local crisis and solves it by going back to a small community, reviving traditions, organising a collective event, and reconciling.
  • Culture on the surface
    Settings, symbols and national references change: fjords and spirits for Norway, olive trees for Palestine and Israel, small towns and old railway stations for the United States. The structure does not.
  • Conflict softened
    Violence and political tension give way to reconciliation. Palestinian stories show the conflict more, but resolve it through peaceful resistance and solidarity.
